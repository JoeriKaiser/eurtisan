# Product

## Register

brand / product (dual-register marketplace; brand surfaces and product surfaces are equally important)

## Users

**Primary:** European creatives, artisans, and makers — ranging from hobbyists selling their first handmade item to established professional artisans with ongoing storefronts.

**Context:** Users browse, discover, and purchase custom merchandise and artisan goods. Makers manage their shops, products, orders, and settings. The platform must serve both the discovery mindset (browsing, inspiration) and the task mindset (shop management, order fulfillment).

**Job to be done:** For buyers — find unique, high-quality handmade goods from European creators. For sellers — reach a European audience, manage a storefront, and grow a creative business.

## Product Purpose

Eurtisan is a European-centered online marketplace for handmade and custom merchandise. It exists to connect European makers with European buyers, with GDPR-conscious architecture, Euro-first pricing, and localization-ready design. Success means both buyers and sellers trust the platform as their go-to European marketplace alternative.

## Brand Personality

**Three words:** Warm, Grounded, Crafted.

**Voice:** Confident but humble. The platform celebrates the maker, not itself. Copy is clear and human, never corporate or hype-driven. European sensibility: refined without being pretentious, approachable without being casual.

**Emotional goals:** Trust, inspiration, belonging. Buyers should feel they're discovering something genuine. Sellers should feel their craft is respected.

## Anti-references

- **No purple or orange** in the palette. These hues are explicitly excluded.
- **No generic bootstrap marketplace look.** Avoid the default "cards in a grid with primary-colored buttons" template feel.
- **Not Etsy visually.** Functionally similar, but Eurtisan must feel completely distinct in identity, color, typography, and atmosphere.
- **No SaaS-cream minimalism.** Avoid the sterile white/gray/blue default that every modern SaaS converges on.
- **No dark-mode-terminal aesthetic.** Dark mode should feel warm and atmospheric, not cold and technical.

## Design Principles

1. **The maker is the hero.** Every design decision should elevate the artisan and their work, not the platform.
2. **Warmth over flash.** The interface should feel like a well-lit workshop, not a billboard. Restrained color, tactile surfaces, human scale.
3. **European sensibility.** Refined typography, generous whitespace, attention to detail. Think quality over quantity.
4. **Trust through craft.** The UI itself should feel crafted — intentional spacing, consistent patterns, no rough edges. If the platform looks hastily built, makers won't trust it with their livelihood.
5. **Accessibility is non-negotiable.** WCAG 2.1 AA is the floor, not the ceiling. The marketplace must work for everyone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA** compliance required for all surfaces.
- **Reduced motion** support for animations and transitions.
- **Color blindness** considerations: never rely on color alone for state or meaning.
- **Localization-ready** UI: support for text expansion, right-to-left languages, and European character sets.
- **Keyboard navigation** fully supported for all interactive elements.
